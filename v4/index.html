The Craft

The Art of Jamdani

Each motif is added by hand, thread by thread, as the fabric is woven — a technique passed down through generations of Bengal’s master weavers.

  • Woven, not printed

    Every motif sits inside the weave itself. Turn the fabric over — there is no wrong side.

  • Made slowly by hand

    A single piece can take weeks on the loom. No two days of weaving are ever the same.

  • Every piece is unique

    Small shifts of the weaver’s hand mean your Jamdani exists nowhere else in the world.
