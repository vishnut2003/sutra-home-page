FAQs

How is Sutra Clothing different from other women's clothing brands online?+

Sutra works directly with handloom artisans across India. Each piece is traceable to a region and a craft. We focus on premium fabrics, fair pricing and pieces that last beyond a single season.

What makes handloom clothing unique?+

Handloom clothing is woven on traditional looms by skilled weavers — no two pieces are ever truly identical. The fabric breathes better, drapes softer, and carries a maker's hand in the weave.

How to style traditional Indian clothing in a modern way?+

Pair a handloom saree with a structured blouse and minimal jewellery; layer a kurta over jeans; wear a co-ord set as separates. Our journal has detailed guides on each.

Which fabrics are best for skin-friendly ethnic wear?+

Pure cotton, mul-mul, linen and chanderi are all skin-friendly, breathable choices. They work especially well in Indian summers.

What type of Indian clothing is best for office wear?+

Look for crisp cottons, structured kurta sets, mul-mul sarees and co-ords in solid or subtle prints. Our "Office" edit in the Saree Edit section is a good place to start.

What is sustainable fashion in Indian ethnic wear?+

It means natural fibres, low-impact dyes, fair wages for artisans, and pieces designed to be worn for many years rather than a single season.

Is handloom clothing worth the price?+

Yes. Handloom takes more time, more skill, and more raw material than power-loom equivalents — and the result lasts longer, ages better and supports a generations-old craft economy.

Final Thought

At Sutra, we believe clothing is a quiet but lasting form of self-expression. Each piece is built to be worn, washed, layered and loved — for a long time. Thank you for letting us be part of your wardrobe.
