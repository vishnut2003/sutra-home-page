Made by hand

Printed by the women of Bagru

Each motif on the Everyday Kurta is carved from teak, dipped in natural madder and pressed by hand onto hand-woven cloth. A single kurta passes through seven hands before it finds you — from the weaver to the dyer to the printer to the tailor.

“We print only with what the earth gives us — madder for red, indigo for blue, pomegranate for yellow. It is slow work, but it is honest work.” — Rajesh Chhipa, Master Printer
