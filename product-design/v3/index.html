Slow notes

A piece is more than a piece.

A saree, in its making, is a quiet act of community. Here is how this one came to be.

01

The fabric

Hand-spun, hand-woven cotton.

110 GSM cotton, hand-spun in Bhuj and pit-loom woven near Maheshwar. Slightly slubby, breathably airy — the kind of cloth that drapes better with every wear.

  • 110 GSM mid-weight cotton
  • Charkha hand-spun yarn
  • Selvedge-finished pallu
02

The print

Carved teak, natural pigments.

Every motif is carved by hand from teak blocks, dipped in madder for the deep reds and in pomegranate-rind for the yellows. The slight irregularities are not flaws — they are the print's signature.

  • Hand-carved teak blocks
  • Madder & pomegranate dyes
  • 7 colour passes per saree
03

The maker

Rajesh & the women of Bagru.

Four generations of natural-dye printers shape every motif you see. A saree passes through seven hands before it finds you. We pay each artisan a fair wage and credit them by name.

  • Fair-wage certified
  • Made-to-order, no stockpiles
  • Artisan credited on tag

“We print only with what the earth gives us. It is slow work, but it is honest work.”

Rajesh Chhipa · Master Printer, Bagru
