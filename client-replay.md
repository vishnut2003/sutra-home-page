## Website Feedback

I recommend this homepage order:

**Announcement → Hero Banner → Shop by Category → New Arrivals → Jamdani Story → Bestsellers → Shop by Craft → Artisan Story → Customer Reviews → Instagram → Newsletter → Footer**

### Navigation

Keep it simple:

**NEW ARRIVALS | SAREES | KURTA SETS | CO-ORDS & SKORDS | DRESSES | TOPS | CRAFT | SALE**

The website should focus more on **SUTRA products and craft**, not look like a generic Indian fashion brand.

### Hero Banner

Instead of only a collection name, clearly tell customers what SUTRA is:

**HANDWOVEN FOR TODAY**
**The New Jamdani Edit**
*Needle-woven by hand. Designed for modern wardrobes.*
**SHOP NEW ARRIVALS**

### Product Section

After the hero, show:

**Kurta Sets | Jamdani | Dresses | Co-ords/Skords | Tops**

Then show **6–8 New Arrival products with price and quick shop options.**

### Jamdani Story

Add a strong section:

**THE ART OF JAMDANI**

* **Woven, not printed**
* **Made slowly by hand**
* **Every piece is unique**

### Mobile

Mobile should be the main focus. Customers should quickly see:

**Logo → Hero → Shop Now → Trust Points → Categories → Products**

### Customer Trust

Add customer photos, reviews and important information like:

**XS–6XL | Easy Exchange | Worldwide Shipping | COD**

### Final Direction

**Keep the premium look of V4, but make it more shopping-focused.**

Visually: **70% premium brand + 30% commerce**
Functionally: **70% shopping + 30% storytelling**

A customer should immediately understand **what SUTRA sells, why it is special, and where to shop.**
